No Gradients.
No shadows.

This layout adheres entirely to the flat design paradigm. Illustrations like those on the right strengthen the visual impact of flat design. Elements rely on solid colors, crisp typography, and generous negative space to establish visual hierarchy. By removing gradients and drop shadows from the content layer, the complex, transparent blur of the glassmorphic navigation bar stands out in striking contrast as it passes over the edges of these shapes.
